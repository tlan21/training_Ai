## Defect found and corrected

The previous screening report overstated its punctuation check as complete sentences. Its ending regex accepted `...`, `..` and Unicode `…`, including endings followed by closing quotes/parentheses. The old packet contains **50 train +3 validation summaries** with these endings. Some are clearly cut-off teasers, such as audit card7 ending in “rồi quyết toán...”, while the body continues with the payment and embezzlement details. Punctuation cannot establish semantic completeness, so this is a conservative rejection rule, not automatic reconstruction of the missing text.

The shared `has_sentence_ending` guard now rejects those terminal endings and preserves internal ellipses in otherwise punctuated text. The trainer rechecks prepared labels before human-audit/GPU work, so the old packet fails with `53 summaries have invalid sentence endings`. Tests cover Unicode/quotes/parentheses, internal ellipses, and refusal at the training entrypoint.

No source or target was rewritten by a model. Existing editor-authored target text was either retained or rejected by the mechanical rule. Human `reviewer` / `verdict` fields were not filled by the agent.
