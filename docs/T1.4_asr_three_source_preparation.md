## Checkpoint selection

Before updates, evaluate candidate133 on full validation for all three domains on the same GPU/precision. The unchanged133 head must reproduce that baseline. Save this initial state as step0.

At each validation, a checkpoint is eligible only when:

1. WER and CER for **every source** are no worse than their own initial baseline.
2. English WER strictly improves.
3. The unweighted mean of the three domain macroWER values strictly improves.

Select the lowest mean validation WER among eligible checkpoints. An ineligible result returns the initial selection score, preventing it from replacing either step0 or an eligible best. History records real domain metrics, raw mean WER and eligibility. No acceptable update leaves step0 intact; `last.pt` may contain a rejected final state and must not be used as the selected checkpoint. Tests exercise the end of the training loop, including this fallback.

Only after checkpoint selection finishes, evaluate selected vs initial on the fixed English/Vietnamese test sets for the paired report. Test values do not choose weights. This is one seed1337 candidate experiment; the project's three-seed final evaluation and real-domain human assessment remain separate requirements. Read-English improvement does not prove Vietnamese-accented English, legal identifiers, numbers or named entities in news audio.
